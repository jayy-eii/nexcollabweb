HOW NEXCOLLAB WORKS

From Sign-Up To Live Collab.

One simple journey — built so influencers get discovered for exactly what they're good at, and brands never have to guess who to work with.

01

Influencer Registers

Creator signs up on NexCollab and sets up a profile — niche, audience size, past work, everything a brand needs to know at a glance.

02

Profile Goes Live

The influencer's profile becomes discoverable in the feed — searchable by category, so the right brands can actually find them.

03

Brand Finds & Approaches

Whenever a brand needs a specific kind of influencer, they search by niche and directly approach the creators who fit their campaign.

04

Collab & Promote — Both Win

Influencer and brand team up on the promotion. The brand gets genuine reach, the influencer gets paid work — a real win-win.
